Source: National Health Service (NHS), Skin rashes in children / urgent advice
Reference: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/rashes-babies-and-children/
Local summary (educational; check the linked source for current guidance):

A rash has many possible causes, and appearance alone may not establish the cause. Seek urgent medical help for a rash with severe illness, breathing difficulty, confusion, a seizure, or other rapidly worsening symptoms. Get professional advice for a rash near the eyes, a very young infant, pregnancy, immune suppression, or persistent/high fever. Contact local healthcare services for advice appropriate to your location. This file is a short reference summary, not a substitute for current NHS guidance or medical care.
